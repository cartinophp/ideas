## 💡 Community & Feedback

We strongly believe in **community-driven development**.
Your feedback and ideas help shape the future of [CartinoPHP](https://github.com/cartinophp/cartino).

Feel free to suggest:

* new **features**
* improvements
* integrations
* ideas for **addons** or extensions

Just open an **issue** and share your idea.
If a proposal reaches **10 votes**, it will definitely be reviewed by our development team.

Depending on its scope and impact, the idea may be:

* implemented directly in the **core**
* or developed as a **separate addon**

Let’s build [CartinoPHP](https://github.com/cartinophp/cartino) together, one feature at a time 🚀
